Install LotKeys

Your dealership workspace on the Home Screen.

One tap from your phone or PCThis installs the current LotKeys web app. It opens in its own app window and updates from the official LotKeys release.
Open LotKeys
1
Android ChromeOpen Chrome’s ⋮ menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen if the blue button is unavailable.
2
iPhone or iPadOpen in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
3
PC Chrome or EdgeUse the install icon at the right side of the address bar.
