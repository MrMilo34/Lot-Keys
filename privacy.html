LotKeys Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026 · Team-testing release

LotKeys helps dealership teams maintain vehicle profiles, prepare Marketplace listings, store account preferences, and communicate with approved users. This policy describes the current browser-based test application.

Information LotKeys uses

LotKeys may use your Google account identifier and email address, your LotKeys display name, optional phone number and profile photo, Store membership, vehicle and listing information, saved posting locations, awards, and Chat content you choose to create.

Where information is stored

The current application stores working data in your browser and stores portable account or dealership data in Google Drive files that you or Store administration authorize. The static application is delivered through GitHub Pages. Do not place customer financial information, passwords, payment-card details, or other data that LotKeys does not request into free-text fields.

Google account and Drive access

LotKeys uses Google Identity Services and Google Drive APIs only after you authorize access. V0.9.4.68 requests Google Drive access during controlled team testing so a Store Code can locate an existing shared Store folder and LotKeys can create, find, read, update, and organize the account and Store files used by its workflows. OAuth Test User approval does not grant access to a Store folder; Store administration must separately approve and provision your Google account. Ordinary and Trusted users receive view access to official Inventory plus write access to their own Listings, More, and Messaging workspace. Public release requires the production security review and narrower authorization described in the release documentation.

Chat and calls

Current Chat message envelopes are encrypted in the browser and relayed through the test Store/Drive design. Voice and video features use browser media and peer-connection capabilities when you actively start or accept a call. The team-test implementation has not completed an independent production security audit and should not be used for highly sensitive communications.

Facebook Post Buddy

The optional Chrome extension reads a Listing selected from an open LotKeys tab and helps fill an open Facebook Marketplace form. It does not press Facebook’s final Next or Publish action. Facebook is a separate service with its own policies.

Sharing and retention

Store data is visible according to Google Drive permissions and LotKeys Store roles. A conversation may remain in a participant’s own archive after that participant leaves a Store. You can remove local site data through browser settings and remove personal LotKeys files from Google Drive. Store-held records may require Store administration to remove them.

Your choices

You may leave the optional phone number blank, decline Google permissions, disconnect from a Store, remove the local Lock Screen, clear browser data, or stop using the application. Features that need a declined permission will not work.

Security and changes

LotKeys uses HTTPS on its published site and continues to add access control, audit, rate-limit, and server-side authorization protections before public launch. This policy may change as those services are introduced.
