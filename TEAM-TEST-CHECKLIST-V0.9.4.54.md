# LotKeys V0.9.4.54 Dev Tool Kit and Post Buddy checklist

Use approved test accounts and non-sensitive dealership data. Test once on a phone and once on a PC before replacing the current team build.

## Release

- Open `https://lot-keys.ca/?build=09454` in a private window and confirm the version bar says V0.9.4.54.
- Confirm all files from V0.9.4.53 remain present, including the 31 transparent Award images and the versioned Post Buddy V0.1.13 ZIP.

## Dev Tool Kit

- Sign in with a Developer-authorized account and open Garage.
- Confirm the toolkit contains Store-wide Celebration and Special Award Drop, with no duplicate Special Award Builder or stand-alone preview button.
- Open Store-wide Celebration, check All users in Store, and confirm your own account remains unchecked.
- Check your own account manually and confirm it can still be included.
- Enter a headline and message, choose an optional Award, and select Preview Celebration.
- Confirm the preview shows the composed content and effect, then returns to the editor without creating a Store event or granting an Award.
- Send to selected test accounts and confirm only the checked recipients receive the event.
- Repeat the selection and preview checks with Special Award Drop.

## Post Buddy

- On a phone, confirm the Garage card says FaceBook Post Buddy · PC only and contains no Open LotKeys Website action.
- On a PC, confirm the card offers Download Post Buddy ZIP and explains that the ZIP must be extracted and loaded through Chrome Extensions with Developer mode enabled.
- Confirm the PC download follows `extension/latest.json` and downloads the versioned V0.1.13 ZIP.
- As Admin Level 2, confirm FaceBook Post Buddy Release contains only the release pointer URL and no Public LotKeys website URL field.

## Regression checks

- Confirm Award images remain transparent and repeat counters stay at the upper-right edge.
- Confirm Pending Deal ribbons remain visible in the user’s Accent Color after direct application or approval.
- Confirm Account editing, Store connection, Inventory, Listings and Chat still open normally.
